# Staff guide

Sign in at `/admin`. You need your password and the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. You are signed out after 15 minutes of no activity, and when you close or reload the tab.

## A report's journey

1. **Reports.** New reports appear with state _new_. Open one. You see the answers and the written account. The phone number is hidden: press **Show phone number** only when you are about to call (each press is recorded with your name).
2. **Call.** Phone the reporter. Log the result under **Call-back log**. _Reached and confirmed_ makes the report _verified_. _No answer_, _wrong number_ and _declined_ leave it _unverified_. You can log as many calls as needed.
3. **Duplicates.** If the report matches an earlier one (same account or group written differently), a **Possible duplicates** box appears. Merge it into the earlier case, or keep it separate. The earlier report must be accepted first.
4. **Accept or reject.** Only a verified report can be accepted. Accepting creates a case number such as SL-2026-0007.
5. **Cases.** Open the case, choose the authority, enter the date you sent it, and press **Record as sent**. From then on, record each reply with its true date, and the status follows. Closing needs an outcome and, if unresolved, a reason.
6. **Follow-ups.** The Follow-ups tab lists cases where an authority has been silent for 14, 30 or 60 days with a draft message that contains no personal details. Press **I sent it** after sending.
7. **Publishing.** In the case, write the short public summary. The checker blocks links, emails, @handles, phone-like numbers and titles followed by names. It cannot catch every name, so read it yourself. When there are two or more staff, a different person must approve before it can be published.

## Screenshots

They are shown blurred and with location and camera data removed. Opening one is recorded. If any image shows someone who may be under 18, press **Quarantine**: nobody can open it again. Then follow `docs/RUNBOOK.md` and tell the owner immediately. Do not save, copy or forward the image.

## Rules

- Never put names, phone numbers, links or screenshots in notes or the public summary.
- Never discuss a report outside this system.
- Download a dossier only to send it to an authority. It needs a fresh code, and it is recorded.
- Under-18 cases are never entered here. The Intake tab has a counter for referrals: it is only a number.
- If you think an account has been compromised, tell the owner at once; they can remove it from **Staff**.
